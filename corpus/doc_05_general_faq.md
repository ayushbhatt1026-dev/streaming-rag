# Doc_05: General Workshop FAQ

## Section 1: Parking

Riverside Hall (Venue A) has on-site parking for up to 15 vehicles, allocated on a
first-come basis. Skyline Conference Center (Venue B) has a paid parking garage
attached to the building, billed separately from the venue booking. Northgate Studio
(Venue C) has no dedicated parking; attendees are directed to a public lot two blocks
away.

## Section 2: Audio-Visual Equipment

All three venues include a projector or large display screen, a wireless microphone,
and standard HDMI/USB-C connectivity as part of the base booking. Requests for
additional AV equipment, such as multi-camera recording setups, must be arranged
separately and are not covered by the standard venue booking.

## Section 3: Accessibility

Riverside Hall and Skyline Conference Center are both wheelchair accessible with
step-free entry and an accessible restroom on the same floor as the workshop room.
Northgate Studio's accessibility accommodations are not documented in this corpus;
bookers with accessibility requirements for Northgate Studio should contact the venue
coordination team directly for current information.

## Section 4: Weekend Bookings

This corpus does not document whether weekend bookings are available at any of the
three venues, or whether a weekend surcharge applies. This is a known gap in the
current documentation set.
