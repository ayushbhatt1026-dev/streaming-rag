# Doc_04: Employee Travel Reimbursement Policy

## Section 1: Standard Domestic Reimbursement Rule

Employees travelling domestically for an approved workshop or business trip may
claim reimbursement for economy-class transport, standard hotel accommodation up to
the department's nightly cap, and meals up to a fixed daily per-diem. Claims must be
submitted with itemized receipts within 14 calendar days of the trip's end date.
No pre-approval is required for domestic trips under the standard caps.

## Section 2: International Travel Exception

If a trip includes international travel, the standard domestic reimbursement rule in
Section 1 still applies as a baseline, but international trips additionally require
senior director approval before the trip is booked. International trips also
require all receipts to be submitted with a foreign currency conversion statement
showing the exchange rate used, in addition to the standard itemized receipts.

## Section 3: Post-Travel Booking Exception

If flight or hotel booking occurs after the travel dates have already begun (a
"post-travel booking"), this is treated as an exception requiring senior director
approval regardless of whether the trip is domestic or international. Post-travel
bookings must include a written justification memo attached to the reimbursement
claim explaining why booking could not occur in advance.

## Section 4: Combined Exceptions

When a trip is both international and involves a post-travel booking, both Section 2
and Section 3 requirements apply together: senior director approval is required
once (not twice), but both the foreign currency conversion statement and the written
justification memo must be included in the same claim submission.
