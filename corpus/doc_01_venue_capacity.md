# Doc_01: Workshop Venue Directory

## Section 1: Venue A — Riverside Hall (Pune)

Venue A, Riverside Hall, is located in Pune and is the primary recommended venue for
mid-sized corporate workshops. The hall has a maximum standing capacity of 120 people
and a maximum seated (classroom-style) capacity of 30 people. Seated capacity is the
relevant number for structured training workshops, since attendees require desks.

Riverside Hall includes one main workshop room, a small breakout room for groups of
up to 10, and a shared reception area. The venue does not include an on-site kitchen,
so catering must be arranged externally (see Doc_09 for catering options).

Booking Riverside Hall requires at least 5 business days of advance notice. Same-day
bookings are not accepted under any circumstance.

## Section 2: Venue B — Skyline Conference Center (Pune)

Venue B, Skyline Conference Center, is a larger alternative venue also located in
Pune, roughly 12 minutes from Riverside Hall. Skyline supports a seated capacity of
up to 60 people in its main hall, and up to 150 people if the partition wall between
the main hall and the adjoining room is removed.

Skyline Conference Center has an in-house catering partner and can arrange food
service directly through the venue (see Doc_09, Section 2, for Skyline's in-house
catering terms).

Booking Skyline requires at least 10 business days of advance notice due to its
higher demand.

## Section 3: Venue C — Northgate Studio (Mumbai)

Venue C, Northgate Studio, is located in Mumbai and is intended for workshops whose
attendees are primarily based in Mumbai rather than Pune. Northgate Studio has a
seated capacity of 40 people. It has no in-house catering; external catering vendors
must be arranged separately, and Northgate Studio requires 48 hours' notice for any
external vendor to be granted building access.

## Section 4: Choosing Between Venues by Group Size

For groups of 30 or fewer attendees requiring seated, classroom-style workshops in
Pune, Venue A (Riverside Hall) is the standard recommendation. For groups larger than
30 but no more than 60 in Pune, Venue B (Skyline Conference Center) is recommended.
For any Pune group larger than 60, Skyline's partition-removed configuration (up to
150) should be requested explicitly at booking time. For Mumbai-based groups of 40 or
fewer, Venue C (Northgate Studio) is the standard recommendation.
