# Doc_02: Workshop Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Section 1: General Cancellation Window

Workshop bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 7 calendar days before the
scheduled workshop date. Cancellations made within the 7-day window but more than 48
hours before the workshop are subject to a 25% cancellation fee, calculated against
the total quoted booking cost.

Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled workshop are subject to a 75%
cancellation fee. Cancellations made after the workshop's scheduled start time are
not eligible for any refund.

## Section 2: Venue-Specific Cancellation Terms

Venue A (Riverside Hall) follows the general cancellation window described in
Section 1 without modification.

Venue B (Skyline Conference Center) requires an additional 10% non-refundable
deposit at time of booking, separate from the cancellation fee schedule in Section 1.
This deposit is never refunded regardless of cancellation timing.

Venue C (Northgate Studio) follows the general cancellation window described in
Section 1, except that the 48-hour cutoff is extended to 72 hours due to Northgate's
own vendor-notice requirements.

## Section 3: Rescheduling Instead of Cancelling

A workshop may be rescheduled to a new date, instead of cancelled, at no additional
fee, provided the request is made at least 5 business days before the original
scheduled date and the new date is confirmed as available at the same venue.
Rescheduling requests made with less than 5 business days' notice are treated as a
cancellation under Section 1 for fee purposes, and a new booking must be made
separately.

## Section 4: Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment
method. Refunds are not available as cash or venue credit under any circumstance.
