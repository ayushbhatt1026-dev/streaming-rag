# Doc_03: Workshop Catering Options

## Section 1: External Catering (Venue A and Venue C)

Riverside Hall (Venue A) and Northgate Studio (Venue C) have no in-house catering.
Bookers must arrange an external catering vendor directly. The venue coordination
team can provide a list of three pre-approved local vendors upon request, but booking
and payment for external catering is handled entirely outside of the venue booking
system.

External caterers must be given venue access at least 2 hours before the workshop
start time to set up. Northgate Studio (Venue C) additionally requires 48 hours'
advance notice to grant any external vendor building access (see Doc_01, Section 3).

## Section 2: In-House Catering (Venue B only)

Skyline Conference Center (Venue B) offers in-house catering through its own
kitchen. In-house catering must be selected from one of three fixed packages:
Continental Breakfast, Working Lunch, or Full-Day Package (breakfast plus lunch plus
two coffee breaks). In-house catering must be ordered at least 3 business days before
the workshop date and is billed directly through the venue invoice, not separately.

## Section 3: Dietary Accommodations

Both external and in-house catering options can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and
gluten-free requests if specified at least 48 hours in advance. Requests made with
less notice cannot be guaranteed and are handled on a best-effort basis only.

## Section 4: Catering Cancellation

For in-house catering at Venue B, cancelling catering separately from the venue
booking is not possible — catering follows the same cancellation schedule as the
venue booking itself (see Doc_02, Section 1). For external catering at Venue A or
Venue C, cancellation terms are set by the external vendor directly and are not
covered by this policy document.
